Nvidia in Talks to Acquire Open-Weights Startup Reflection AI

Nvidia is weighing a buyout, deeper investment, or acqui-hire of open-weights model developer Reflection AI to expand beyond semiconductor hardware.

In short

  • Nvidia is engaged in early-stage talks to acquire, deepen investment in, or acqui-hire open-weights startup Reflection AI.
  • Reflection AI, founded by former Google DeepMind researchers in 2024, recently sought funding at a $25 billion valuation.
  • Potential transaction structures include an acqui-hire agreement designed to secure engineering talent while avoiding protracted regulatory reviews.
  • Nvidia has already committed $800 million to Reflection AI, which recently launched its Beam coding model.

Nvidia is in preliminary discussions to acquire or increase its investment in Reflection AI, a U.S.-based developer of open-weight artificial intelligence models, according to a report by the Financial Times cited by Investing.com. The discussions could expand the chipmaker's reach into software agents and developer tools. The negotiations signal a strategic evolution for the semiconductor giant as it builds out support for models that run directly on enterprise hardware.

Potential Deal Structures and Regulatory Strategies

According to reporting from the Financial Times covered by PYMNTS, discussions between the two companies are currently in an early phase. The talks encompass several prospective paths forward, including a standard corporate acquisition, a deepened equity stake, or an agreement to provide additional computing power and semiconductors.

Another option under consideration is an "acqui-hire" arrangement. As noted by Seeking Alpha, this approach would enable Nvidia to recruit Reflection AI's research personnel and license its technology while avoiding the protracted antitrust and regulatory reviews typically associated with full takeovers. People familiar with the situation told reporters that a deal could be completed in the coming weeks, though both parties could still walk away without reaching a formal agreement.

Startup Background and Valuation Context

Reflection AI was launched in 2024 by former Google DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou. The company focuses on developing automated software engineering tools, positioning its products in the market for autonomous coding agents and developer assistants.

Nvidia is already an established backer of the startup, having previously invested $800 million, according to details compiled by Dealroom. In April, Reflection AI Chief Executive Officer Misha Laskin told CNBC that the company was raising capital at a $25 billion pre-money valuation.

The discussions follow Reflection AI's recent release of "Beam," its first open-weight AI model designed specifically for coding and agentic workloads. Beam was developed to provide an alternative to low-cost systems originating in China, such as DeepSeek and Kimi, targeting developers who require cost-effective weights for complex software tasks.

Enterprise Software Push and Strategic Outlook

Moving deeper into software development models would complement Nvidia's prevailing role in providing the hardware accelerators used to train and run frontier models. As reported by PYMNTS, Nvidia has focused increasingly in recent months on supporting an open-weight ecosystem where organizations can customize weights and run workloads on private infrastructure, reducing reliance on hosted proprietary services.

Neither Nvidia nor Reflection AI immediately responded to requests for comment regarding the discussions, and Reuters reported it could not independently confirm the talks. If concluded, the agreement would expand Nvidia's vertical presence across both the hardware infrastructure and the underlying model layers powering enterprise software automation.

Questions people ask

What is Reflection AI?

Reflection AI is an American startup founded in 2024 by former Google DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou. It builds open-weight AI models and autonomous software development agents, including its coding model named Beam.

How much has Nvidia invested in Reflection AI so far?

Nvidia has committed $800 million in prior funding to Reflection AI, which previously sought financing at a $25 billion pre-money valuation.

Why is an acqui-hire structure being considered?

An acqui-hire structure allows Nvidia to license Reflection AI's technology and hire its engineering and research talent while circumventing lengthy antitrust and regulatory reviews tied to full corporate buyouts.
