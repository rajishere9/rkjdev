DeepSeek Raises $15 Billion in Round Backed by CATL and Tencent

The Hangzhou-based AI firm secured approximately 100 billion yuan from industrial and tech giants as it scales computing infrastructure for an eventual public listing.

In short

  • DeepSeek secured roughly 100 billion yuan ($15 billion) in new capital after raising its fundraising target twice.
  • Battery maker CATL and tech conglomerate Tencent were among the round's largest financial backers.
  • The capital injection brings DeepSeek's six-month fundraising total to $20.6 billion at a $68.8 billion pre-money valuation.
  • Proceeds are designated for model research, computing power, and corporate restructuring ahead of a planned IPO.

Hangzhou-based artificial intelligence startup DeepSeek has raised approximately 100 billion yuan ($15 billion) in a massive funding round co-led by battery manufacturer Contemporary Amperex Technology Co. (CATL) and WeChat parent Tencent Holdings, according to reporting by Bloomberg News. The transaction ranks among the largest primary market financings in Chinese corporate history, supplying the firm with essential capital as it expands domestic frontier artificial intelligence infrastructure.

DeepSeek first commanded global attention in 2025 after proving it could build competitive artificial intelligence models at significantly lower costs than Western peers, a development that prompted a broad sell-off across international technology equities. The startup's latest financing underlines how major domestic industrial and technology corporations are pooling balance-sheet resources to underwrite the compute and infrastructure requirements of frontier model development.

Escalating Targets and Investor Lineup

DeepSeek expanded its fundraising ambitions several times before reaching the final $15 billion figure. According to reporting from GuruFocus and Bloomberg News via Investing.com, the startup initially pursued a 50 billion yuan target, subsequently raised the goal to 80 billion yuan, and finally closed at approximately 100 billion yuan (roughly $14.98 billion to $15 billion).

Investment data compiled by Dealroom indicates that DeepSeek's pre-money valuation held steady at approximately $68.8 billion during the process. The deal features an even distribution between existing and new backers. CATL doubled down on its position by committing approximately $1.38 billion, following an earlier deployment of roughly $687.5 million.

Beyond co-leads Tencent and CATL, new participants in the financing include Stony Creek Capital, China Fortune-Tech Capital, CPE Asset Management, Legend Capital, and Geely. Existing institutional shareholders participating in the transaction include IDG Capital, Lux Capital, and Silicon 100, according to Dealroom. Tencent closed at 417.80 HKD (-3.00%) on October 21 and CATL closed down 2.63%, as reported by Investing.com.

Rapid Capital Influx and Computing Infrastructure

The transaction is the second major funding round DeepSeek has pursued in 2026, though Dealroom reports the terms and totals are not yet finalized. The startup finalized a separate funding round of more than $6.88 billion in June before opening this second tranche in July, as tracked by Dealroom. Combined, the two transactions provided DeepSeek with $20.6 billion in primary capital within six months.

The fresh funding is specifically earmarked to support expanded model research and the acquisition of raw computing power. As detailed by Investing.com, DeepSeek has accelerated operational expenditure on enterprise data centers and its suite of open-source models since its initial emergence in 2025.

Global Competition and Public Listing Roadmap

DeepSeek's multibillion-dollar influx arrives amid intensifying capital commitments across the frontier artificial intelligence industry. According to Investing.com and the Economic Times, the financing is intended to narrow the operational resource gap separating DeepSeek from premier United States AI laboratories. Leading American competitors OpenAI and Anthropic have each raised upwards of $100 billion, leaving DeepSeek operating with comparatively smaller aggregate financial reserves despite its recent success.

Domestic competition is simultaneously accelerating. Chinese peer Moonshot AI has reportedly approached a $50 billion valuation while planning an initial public offering in Hong Kong, according to Dealroom.

DeepSeek is orchestrating its own public markets transition. Reports from GuruFocus state that the $15 billion round directly paves the way for an initial public offering, with regulatory filings possible as early as 2026. Preparations include formal corporate restructuring and groundwork for an initial public offering on Shanghai's Star Market, as reported by Dealroom.

Questions people ask

How much capital did DeepSeek raise in this funding round?

DeepSeek raised approximately 100 billion yuan, or roughly $15 billion (reported as $13.8 billion to $14.98 billion across sources), after elevating its target from an initial 50 billion yuan.

Who are the major investors participating in the round?

Battery manufacturer CATL and Tencent co-led and were among the largest backers. Other participants include Stony Creek Capital, China Fortune-Tech Capital, CPE Asset Management, Legend Capital, Geely, IDG Capital, Lux Capital, and Silicon 100.

What will DeepSeek use the $15 billion for?

The proceeds are designated for advanced model research, computing power, expanded data center capacity, open-source model development, and corporate restructuring ahead of a potential IPO.

Where does DeepSeek intend to go public?

Reports indicate DeepSeek is preparing for an initial public offering on Shanghai's Star Market, with listing filings potentially occurring as early as 2026.
