Apple Releases iOS 27.2 With Siri AI Upgrades and Five Languages

The update expands Siri AI to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish, while introducing conversation recall and Apple Pencil support for iPhone Duo.

In short

  • iOS 27.2 expands Siri AI beta support to French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish on compatible devices.
  • The virtual assistant adds conversation recall, deeper personal context integration across Messages and Calendar, and an Excluded Apps privacy control.
  • The iPhone Duo gains Apple Pencil (USB-C) compatibility across both displays alongside an on-screen Edge Light feature.
  • Siri AI remains unavailable in the European Union and mainland China, and Apple notes that server-backed features face daily limits with paid tiers planned.

On October 26, 2026, Apple officially released iOS 27.2 alongside iPadOS 27.2, macOS 27.2, watchOS 27.2, and visionOS 27.2. The software deployment marks the first major update cycle since the September debut of the iOS 27 operating system, bringing a substantial expansion to the company's Siri AI assistant. According to Apple's release notes published via MacRumors, iOS 27.2 extends Siri AI to five additional global languages, adds conversational memory, integrates deeper contextual indexing across system applications, and activates stylus compatibility on the foldable iPhone Duo.

Siri AI launched in September as an overhaul of the platform's assistant, and this release expands its international footprint and contextual awareness. While earlier builds were restricted to English, Siri AI in iOS 27.2 adds support for French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish. In an official Apple Newsroom announcement, Apple stated that the assistant operates in beta on Apple Intelligence-enabled hardware set to one of the six supported languages.

Siri AI Conversational Recall and Personal Context

The central functional improvement in iOS 27.2 is the addition of conversational recall. According to 9to5Mac's feature breakdown, Siri AI can now retain conversational context across sessions. This allows users to follow up on previous inquiries, such as asking the assistant for details on restaurant recommendations it made during the prior week.

Siri AI also pulls from a wider range of personal context sources stored across the device. Release notes confirm that the system can now surface details from photos attached inside Messages, calendar event attendee lists, saved voicemails, Safari browsing history, and Reading List entries. To give users direct oversight over these indexes, Apple introduced an Excluded Apps menu within settings, allowing individuals to block specific applications from appearing in Siri, Search, and system suggestions.

Additionally, the update includes granular text selection for Siri interactions, letting users highlight and copy specific sections of a generated response rather than copying the entire text block. On the wearable front, Apple stated in 9to5Mac that watchOS 27.2 accelerates Siri AI response times compared to previous builds.

iPhone Duo Gains Apple Pencil Support

For owners of Apple's foldable iPhone Duo, iOS 27.2 introduces hardware capabilities promised during the device's announcement. As reported by PhoneArena, the update enables support for the Apple Pencil (USB-C) on both the inner and outer displays, marking the first time an iPhone has supported an Apple-branded stylus. Apple notes that Apple Pencil Pro and other stylus models remain unsupported.

The foldable device also receives Edge Light, a display feature previously seen on macOS that illuminates the perimeter of the screen to light a user's face during video calls. Other system-level communication upgrades documented by 9to5Mac include Dual Capture in Group FaceTime, allowing users to broadcast from both the front and rear cameras at the same time, as well as an option detailed in Apple's release notes to add a live captioner or third-party sign language interpreter to calls.

Systemwide Changes, SynthID, and Smart Home Controls

Beyond Siri AI and foldable hardware, iOS 27.2 implements modifications across Apple's core apps. Messages introduces Suggestions, providing action shortcuts based on text context, such as prompting the creation of a Calendar invite or Reminder when discussing dinner plans. The Photos app integrates support for Google's SynthID watermarking technology to identify images generated or altered with artificial intelligence tools across Photos and Image Playground. Shared Albums in Photos also adds comment editing, caption editing, duplicate photo prevention, and album expiration notifications.

In media applications, Apple Music features a redesigned black-and-white icon, blue tab accents, and black widget backgrounds. Apple Podcasts divides its Home tab into New Episodes and Continue Playing sections, replacing the previous Up Next queue. The Apple TV app introduces Simple Profiles on iPhone, allowing individual household members to maintain isolated recommendations and watch histories.

Smart home adjustments in the Home app include Lock After Closing and Door Left Open alerts for compatible smart door locks, alongside a Lock Controls toggle for HomePod speakers to prevent unintended playback triggers while preserving alarm and timer functions. A redesigned Health app seen in early developer betas was pulled before release and remains planned for later in 2026, according to 9to5Mac.

Device Requirements, Geographic Limits, and Paid Cloud Tiers

Access to Siri AI requires specific hardware. As outlined in Apple's release documentation, the advanced assistant features operate exclusively on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and all iPhone 16 models and later. Standard iOS 27.2 system features remain available on all phones capable of running iOS 27, extending from iPhone 11 up to the flagship lineup.

Geographic and policy constraints continue to limit Apple Intelligence deployments. Siri AI is unavailable in mainland China and the European Union across iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS. In its Newsroom release, Apple stated that it is "working constructively with the EU in an attempt to find a path forward." For EU users, iOS 27.2 adds an alternative expanded prompt for App Tracking Transparency. Apple also specifies that Siri AI is not available to accounts registered to users under 13 years of age.

Apple highlighted that features utilizing server-side models—including Siri AI, intelligent photo editing tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts—are subject to daily usage limits based on request complexity and system demand. The company confirmed in its support terms that expanded access to these server-based capabilities will be offered for a fee in the future. Eligible users can install the update immediately via Settings > General > Software Update.

Questions people ask

Which languages does Siri AI support in iOS 27.2?

iOS 27.2 supports English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish on Apple Intelligence-enabled devices. Additional languages including traditional Chinese, Norwegian, Turkish, and Vietnamese are scheduled for later release.

Which iPhone models support the new Siri AI features?

Siri AI features require an iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 model or later. Other iOS 27.2 features, such as app redesigns and watermarking support, are available across all iOS 27-compatible models.

Why is Siri AI unavailable in the European Union?

Siri AI is blocked in the EU on iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS. Apple stated in its Newsroom update that it is working constructively with the EU to find a path forward.

What Apple Pencil models work with the iPhone Duo?

Only the Apple Pencil (USB-C) is supported on the iPhone Duo. Apple Pencil Pro and other stylus models are not compatible.
