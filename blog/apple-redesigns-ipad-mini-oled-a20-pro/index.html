Apple Unveils Redesigned iPad Mini With OLED and A20 Pro Chip

The updated compact tablet gains an 8.5-inch OLED panel, IP67 ingress protection, and an A20 Pro chip with tier-dependent neural hardware.

In short

  • The 2026 iPad mini features an 8.5-inch OLED panel reaching 1,200 nits peak brightness, housed in a slimmer 5.4 mm chassis.
  • Apple introduced an IP67 water and dust resistance rating and an Acoustic Display audio system built into the screen assembly.
  • The A20 Pro chip configuration varies by storage tier: 512GB models include an extra CPU core and a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, while 128GB and 256GB models receive a binned variant.
  • Pricing starts at $699 for Wi-Fi and $849 for cellular, with pre-orders beginning October 28, 2026, and in-store availability on November 6, 2026.

Apple introduced an overhauled iPad mini on October 26, 2026, replacing the previous generation's display with an 8.5-inch OLED screen and equipping the tablet with the company's flagship A20 Pro processor. According to the official Apple Newsroom announcement, the updated device features a thinner and lighter chassis, official IP67 water and dust resistance, and an internal speaker system that projects sound directly through the display glass.

The hardware refresh marks a substantial pricing and positioning shift for Apple's smallest tablet. With a starting price of $699—up from the $499 launch price of the 2024 model—silicon tiering distinguishes the top-tier storage capacity from the rest of the lineup.

8.5-Inch OLED Display and Slimmer Form Factor

The most visible change to the hardware is the transition from liquid crystal to an OLED panel. The screen measures 8.5 inches diagonally, expanding slightly from the 8.3-inch diagonal on the 2024 model. As reported by Engadget, Apple achieved the larger screen area by trimming the surrounding display bezels by 24 percent rather than increasing the tablet's exterior footprint.

Apple claims the panel reaches a peak brightness of 1,200 nits for HDR content—more than double the previous model's peak—and sustains 600 nits across full-screen SDR and HDR viewing, backed by a 2,000,000:1 contrast ratio. Customers buying higher-capacity versions can also opt for a nano-texture matte glass coating for an additional $100. However, the display retains a 60Hz refresh rate rather than Apple's 120Hz ProMotion technology, according to 9to5Mac.

Physical dimensions have also been altered. The tablet measures 5.4 mm thick, down from 6.3 mm on the previous iteration, and weighs 255 grams compared to the earlier 297 grams. Apple also sealed the aluminum body to achieve an IP67 rating under IEC standard 60529, allowing it to withstand water immersion up to 1 meter for up to 30 minutes in laboratory conditions.

Silicon Tiering Across the A20 Pro

Processing duties are handled by the A20 Pro system-on-a-chip, which first appeared in the iPhone 18 Pro lineup earlier this year. Apple highlighted the chip's on-device artificial intelligence capabilities, noting the presence of hardware-accelerated ray tracing and dedicated Neural Accelerators embedded inside each GPU core.

However, 9to5Mac noted a significant hardware limitation across different storage configurations. The entry-level 128GB and mid-tier 256GB versions ship with a binned version of the A20 Pro featuring a 5-core CPU (two super cores and three efficiency cores), a 7-core GPU, and a standard 16-core Neural Engine.

Only the top-tier 512GB configuration receives the fully enabled silicon. That model includes a 6-core CPU (two super cores and four efficiency cores) and a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, delivering more local computing throughput for on-device machine learning workflows and Apple Intelligence features.

Acoustic Display, In-House Modems, and Cameras

To accommodate the thinner profile and liquid sealing, Apple engineered an audio subsystem it terms an "Acoustic Display." Apple paired custom woofers directly behind the display layer to output lower frequencies, while hiding tweeters between the front glass and the outer aluminum rim. Daring Fireball noted that this sealed design is central to how Apple secured the IP67 ingress protection rating.

Connectivity features custom Apple silicon throughout. The tablet integrates Apple's N1 networking chip, adding support for Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6, and Thread networking. Cellular models incorporate Apple's in-house C2 cellular modem, which Apple claims provides 50 percent faster data throughput than the cellular modem in the 2024 model.

For cameras, the tablet shifts its 12-megapixel Center Stage front camera from the short top bezel to the landscape edge, aligning it with other modern iPad models for video conferencing. The rear chassis holds a single 12-megapixel camera supporting Smart HDR 4 and 4K video recording at up to 60 frames per second. Biometric authentication remains tied to a Touch ID sensor rather than Face ID sensors.

Apple Intelligence and Siri AI

The hardware is built to run Apple Intelligence, which relies on a hybrid architecture of local model processing and Private Cloud Compute. With iPadOS 27.2, Apple is introducing a beta release of Siri AI, which draws on on-screen awareness and personal contextual data across mail, photos, and messages.

Apple's documentation outlines that certain server-side features will operate under daily usage caps, including Image Playground generation and advanced photo manipulation tools such as upgraded Clean Up, Spatial Reframing, and Extend. Siri AI in iPadOS is not currently available in the European Union, where Apple stated it is continuing regulatory discussions.

Pricing, Accessories, and Availability

The 2026 iPad mini arrives in two finishes: midnight and starlight. According to Apple Newsroom, pre-orders open on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, across 36 countries and regions, with retail availability and customer deliveries scheduled to begin on Friday, November 6, 2026.

Base pricing stands at $699 for the 128GB Wi-Fi model, $800 for 256GB as reported by Daring Fireball, and $999 for the 512GB version that houses the unbinned A20 Pro. Adding cellular connectivity increases the price of any tier by $150, pushing the base cellular model to $849. An education discount reduces the starting price to $649.

For input accessories, the tablet supports both the $79 Apple Pencil (USB-C) and the $129 Apple Pencil Pro. Apple is also introducing a new TechWoven Smart Folio in navy blue and taupe for $79.

Questions people ask

When does the new iPad mini become available?

Pre-orders open on Wednesday, October 28, 2026, with customer deliveries and retail store availability starting Friday, November 6, 2026.

What is the difference between the storage tiers on the new iPad mini?

The 128GB and 256GB models use a binned A20 Pro chip featuring a 5-core CPU and a 16-core Neural Engine. The 512GB model features an unbinned A20 Pro with a 6-core CPU and a Dual 16-core Neural Engine.

Does the new iPad mini support 120Hz ProMotion?

No. Tech specifications confirm that the 8.5-inch OLED display operates at a standard 60Hz refresh rate.

Is the new iPad mini waterproof?

It has an official IP67 rating under IEC standard 60529, meaning it was tested to survive freshwater immersion up to 1 meter deep for up to 30 minutes. It resists splashes and minor dunks, but liquid damage is not covered under warranty.
