TSMC Posts Record Q3 2026 Profit as TSMC and CEO C.C. Wei Say Rising Adoption of Agentic…

The world's largest contract foundry beat forecasts with NT$763.06 billion in profit and raised full-year dollar revenue growth projections to 44%.

In short

  • TSMC reported Q3 2026 net income of NT$763.06 billion ($23.6B to $23.9B), marking a 68.7% increase year-over-year.
  • Chairman and CEO C.C. Wei raised full-year dollar revenue growth guidance to 44%, citing strong demand fueled by the adoption of agentic AI.
  • Advanced technologies accounted for 79% of total wafer revenue, with 3-nanometer processes contributing 33% and 2-nanometer reaching 8%.
  • Fourth-quarter revenue guidance was set between US$52.3 billion and US$54.3 billion, well ahead of consensus estimates.

On October 15, 2026, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. reported its highest quarterly profit to date for the third quarter of 2026. The world's largest contract chipmaker saw net income surge 68.7% year-over-year to NT$763.06 billion, driven by unrelenting global demand for processors powering artificial intelligence infrastructure.

The results extend TSMC's record-setting run to a sixth consecutive quarter of record earnings and mark its eleventh straight quarter of year-over-year profit expansion. The performance indicates that enterprise capital spending on AI computing hardware remains vigorous despite broader market concerns regarding interest rates and technology valuations.

Record Third-Quarter Results Across Advanced Nodes

According to TSMC's third-quarter earnings release, consolidated revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, totaled NT$1,494.24 billion (US$46.72 billion). This represents a 50.9% increase in New Taiwan dollars and a 41.2% rise in U.S. dollar terms compared to the third quarter of 2025. Diluted earnings per share reached NT$29.43, equivalent to US$4.60 per American Depositary Receipt (ADR) unit.

TSMC maintained strong operating efficiency during the period. Gross margin stood at 66.4%, compared with 67.7% in the previous quarter, while operating margin came in at 58.7% and net profit margin reached 51.1%.

Revenue by technology highlighted customers' reliance on TSMC's most advanced process nodes. Advanced technologies—classified by the foundry as 7-nanometer and smaller geometries—accounted for 79% of total wafer revenue in the quarter:

  • 3-nanometer: 33% of wafer revenue
  • 5-nanometer: 28% of wafer revenue
  • 7-nanometer: 10% of wafer revenue
  • 2-nanometer: 8% of wafer revenue

Executive Leadership Highlights Shift Toward Agentic AI

During the company's investor conference, TSMC executives disputed suggestions that customer appetite for artificial intelligence silicon is slowing down. Chairman and Chief Executive Officer C.C. Wei stated that TSMC has seen no sign of a market slowdown, describing ongoing demand as extremely robust.

As reported by Morningstar, Wei explained that hardware needs are evolving as the technology shifts from basic conversational bots to more autonomous software systems. "We observe increasing AI demand fueled by the rising adoption of agentic AI as we transition from chatbot to AI assistance," Wei noted.

Reflecting that sustained demand, Wei raised the company's full-year 2026 revenue growth forecast to 44% in U.S. dollar terms, up from earlier guidance of slightly over 40%. The increase is supported by product differentiation and an expanding customer base.

Strong Fourth-Quarter Outlook and Capital Plans

TSMC provided an upbeat forecast for the final quarter of the year. Chief Financial Officer Wendell Huang stated that the company expects fourth-quarter revenue between US$52.3 billion and US$54.3 billion, assuming an exchange rate of US$1 to NT$31.8. At the midpoint, the guidance implies a 58% year-over-year increase, topping analyst consensus figures of roughly US$51.18 billion.

Fourth-quarter gross profit margin is anticipated to sit between 65% and 67%, with operating profit margin projected between 56.5% and 58.5%. Huang stated that fourth-quarter business is expected to be supported by continued strong demand for leading-edge process technologies, including the steep ramp-up of 2-nanometer technology.

As detailed by CTech, TSMC maintained its full-year 2026 capital expenditure target at US$60 billion to US$64 billion, following an upward revision announced in July. However, Huang stated on the call that the company predicts capital expenditure specifically allocated to AI will be significantly higher between 2026 and 2028 than over the preceding three-year period. Customers also continue to place heavy orders for TSMC's advanced chip-packaging technology, known as CoWoS.

Fab Expansion Timelines and Market Reaction

TSMC provided updates on its overseas manufacturing footprint. The company confirmed that volume production in Arizona will commence in the third quarter of 2027, running slightly ahead of schedule. Reporting on the scale of the Arizona investment diverged among outlets: Morningstar stated TSMC is investing $265 billion following a $100 billion expansion announced in July, whereas CTech cited the total figure at $165 billion.

Broader industry developments also provided a positive backdrop for the foundry's expansion. A day prior to TSMC's release, semiconductor equipment manufacturer ASML reported that customers are already placing tool orders for deliveries in 2029 and 2030.

Despite the record financial results, public markets responded cautiously amid macroeconomic pressures. TSMC's Taipei-listed shares closed down 2% ahead of the release, tracking a broader regional tech sell-off linked to expectations of higher borrowing costs. In New York, the company's ADRs fell 1.5% in premarket trading to $423.30. Even with the intraday drop, TSMC's American shares remain up approximately 44% for 2026 as reported by Morningstar, reflecting the company's position as a key manufacturer of advanced AI chips.

Chairman and CEO C.C. Wei highlighted the transition from conversational chatbots to agentic AI assistants, which has driven sustained demand for advanced wafer manufacturing and CoWoS packaging.

What is TSMC's revenue forecast for the fourth quarter of 2026?

TSMC expects fourth-quarter revenue between US$52.3 billion and US$54.3 billion, representing year-over-year growth of roughly 55% to 61%.
