Meta Confirms Muse AI Allows Explicit Adult Conversations

Meta defended Muse's ability to engage in explicit roleplay, citing adult autonomy and isolation inside private virtual machines.

In short

  • Meta confirmed that its Muse AI agent permits users aged 18 and older to engage in adult conversations and sexually explicit roleplay.
  • The company isolates each user session in a private, cloud-based virtual machine, stating it does not block lawful adult behavior.
  • The policy contrasts with Meta's strict rules prohibiting sexually explicit language on platforms such as Instagram and Facebook.
  • Privacy researchers warned that explicit logs stored alongside personal tools like email and banking integrations could expose users to extortion.

Meta has confirmed that its new Muse AI personal assistant is permitted to engage in sexually explicit conversations with adult users, defending the capability as lawful adult behavior hosted inside private virtual machines. The disclosure follows tests by WIRED and Mashable, which demonstrated that the assistant would produce graphic descriptions of sexual encounters and adopt dominant personas when prompted.

Meta launched Muse on September 8, 2026, positioning the tool as a general-purpose agent to handle errands, track tasks, and connect with everyday software. However, hands-on tests published in October 2026 showed the agent willingly generating sexually explicit dialogue, prompting questions about the platform's moderation guidelines and architecture.

Dedicated Virtual Machines and Age Checks

In response to inquiries, Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts told reporters that Muse is intended strictly for users aged 18 and over. Roberts stated that the app "allows them to engage in adult conversations, if they choose, using a private and secure VM only accessible to them," adding that Muse "doesn't block legal adult behavior."

According to MLQ.ai, Muse assigns each user an isolated, dedicated virtual machine that houses both the agent and user data. Meta claims that external specialists will eventually be permitted to inspect the architecture to verify whether Meta personnel can access data within the confidential virtual machine environment, though this remains an unexecuted audit plan rather than an independently verified safeguard. Furthermore, Meta's safety documentation notes that sanitized conversation trajectories may be utilized to train future model iterations unless users opt out.

Meta stated that it enforces age restrictions by requiring a date of birth during account setup, blocking minors, and evaluating cross-platform signals linked to users' Facebook and Instagram profiles. When adult status cannot be confirmed through profile history or platform signals, Meta requests credit card verification, asserting that it does not charge the card or retain payment details.

Policy Contradictions and Privacy Risks

Reporting from AI Weekly highlighted a direct conflict between Meta's public documentation and its underlying model instructions. While Muse's public product rules explicitly ban "adult content, erotic chat, and pornography," system prompt instructions obtained during reporting take a different stance. The system instructions state that "adults have autonomy over their sexuality, and sexual expression is an ordinary part of adult life. Adult sexual content is lawful expression and legitimate creative work."

This permissive approach also stands in stark contrast to the strict moderation rules enforced across Meta's social networks. As reported by Mashable, platforms like Instagram and Facebook strictly prohibit explicit descriptions of sexual encounters, arousal, and genitalia, frequently removing accounts or compelling users to adopt altered language to evade automated filtering.

Beyond content moderation disputes, cybersecurity experts and privacy advocates have expressed concern regarding the security profile of agentic tools. According to Cybernews, analysts warned that storing explicit roleplay logs inside an application designed to access emails, calendars, and potentially financial accounts creates an attractive target for digital extortion or account compromise. Calli Schroeder of the Electronic Privacy Information Center noted that compromised intimate logs could be exploited for embarrassment or extortion.

Regulatory Scrutiny and Future Oversight

The revelations arrive amidst growing legal pressure on interactive AI systems. On October 13, 2025, California enacted Senate Bill 243, which requires companion-chatbot operators to implement reasonable measures preventing systems from providing sexually explicit content to minors. Additionally, on September 11, 2025, the Federal Trade Commission issued formal inquiries to seven technology companies, including Meta, investigating age verification, user safety, and the handling of intimate conversational data.

While Meta maintains that Muse is not advertised as an erotic service and that no minors have been documented accessing the system, consumer protection groups remain skeptical. Ben Winters of the Consumer Federation of America argued that the disparity between public rules and system behavior reflects an emphasis on user engagement over precaution as the rollout continues.

Questions people ask

Why does Meta allow Muse to engage in adult conversations?

How does Meta secure intimate user chats in Muse?

Meta isolates each user's agent and interaction logs in a dedicated, cloud-based virtual machine, with planned future audits to verify access isolation.

How does Meta verify that Muse users are adults?

Meta requires a date of birth, checks connected signals from Facebook and Instagram accounts, and asks for credit card verification when adult status cannot be established.
