Salesforce to Acquire Braze for $5.7B to Expand Agentforce

The $5.66 billion all-cash deal brings real-time event streaming and AI decisioning to Salesforce's marketing stack and Agentforce.

In short

  • Salesforce has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Braze for approximately $5.66 billion in enterprise value at $47.50 per share in cash.

  • The deal is expected to close in Salesforce's fiscal year 2028, which begins in early February, pending shareholder and regulatory approvals.

Salesforce announced on October 20, 2026, that it has reached a definitive agreement to acquire customer-engagement platform Braze for approximately $5.66 billion in enterprise value. Under the terms of the transaction, holders of Braze Class A common stock will receive $47.50 per share in cash, according to an official Salesforce press release. Braze's real-time engagement and AI decisioning capabilities are expected to complement Agentforce and Marketing Cloud.

The purchase price of $47.50 per share represents an approximate 58% premium over Braze's previous closing price of $30.04, as reported by Investing.com. Following the announcement, trading in Braze stock was temporarily halted before the New York market open, while Salesforce shares dropped roughly 2.6% to 3% in trading sessions that morning.

Integrating Braze into Agentforce and Customer 360

Braze provides a cloud-native, event-streaming system designed to handle high-volume B2C interactions across channels including mobile apps, websites, email, SMS, and WhatsApp. According to Salesforce, Braze processes trillions of messages and journey actions annually. Its BrazeAI system provides algorithmic optimization for delivery timing, channel selection, content, and promotional offers based on live consumer behavior.

Once completed, the acquisition will position Braze's real-time engagement and AI decisioning across four core layers of Salesforce's technology stack: Data 360, Customer 360, Agentforce, and SIForce. Within Customer 360, Braze's technology is slated to bolster Salesforce Marketing Cloud by providing cross-channel personalization for consumer-facing brands that manage audiences numbering in the hundreds of millions.

"Braze was built to turn live customer behavior into personalized engagement at scale," said Patrick Stokes, President of Applications and Marketing at Salesforce, in the release. Stokes added that integrating those capabilities with Salesforce Marketing Cloud, CRM, and Data 360 is intended to improve responsiveness at critical customer touchpoints.

Customer Base and Leadership Retention

Braze currently serves approximately 2,770 customers, including Canva, Dairy Queen, Equinox, Instacart, Urban Outfitters, and Venmo, as detailed by Quartz. The company was co-founded by CEO Bill Magnuson and Jon Hyman, both of whom will bring data engineering and mobile engagement expertise to Salesforce Marketing Cloud.

Magnuson affirmed that the platform will continue its product roadmap under the Braze identity. "This past year we shipped at the fastest pace in our history, and BrazeAI is helping marketers get their best ideas live faster," Magnuson said in the announcement. He noted that Salesforce's scale would help accelerate development for their existing community of enterprise clients and software partners.

Salesforce emphasized that both companies intend to maintain an open, composable approach that lets customers continue utilizing third-party data warehouses, tools, and partners alongside the integrated platform.

Financial Terms, Advisors, and Regulatory Timeline

The transaction is scheduled to close during Salesforce's fiscal year 2028, which begins in early February, as reported by Quartz citing Bloomberg data. The deal remains subject to customary closing conditions, which include regulatory clearances and a formal approval vote by Braze shareholders. Braze plans to file a preliminary proxy statement on Schedule 14A with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

Salesforce stated that the acquisition will not alter its financial guidance for fiscal year 2027. The company has not yet issued guidance for fiscal year 2028 and made no updates to its long-term financial targets.

For advisory support, J.P. Morgan Securities LLC acts as the exclusive financial advisor to Salesforce, with legal counsel provided by Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz alongside Covington & Burling LLP. Qatalyst Partners serves as exclusive financial advisor to Braze, with Davis Polk & Wardwell LLP acting as legal counsel, according to Salesforce. Both companies acknowledged standard transaction risks, including the potential for regulatory interventions, competing acquisition offers, or delays in realizing projected operational efficiencies.
