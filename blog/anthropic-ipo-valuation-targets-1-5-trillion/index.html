Anthropic Faces $1.5 Trillion Valuation Talk as Q3 Revenue Slows

Prospective backers reassess pricing ahead of a planned November debut as sequential revenue growth eases to 45% and distillation revenue shrinks.

In short

  • Investors attending Anthropic's San Francisco briefing discussed a valuation target of roughly $1.5 trillion, stepping down from earlier targets of $1.8 trillion to $2 trillion.
  • Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 45% sequentially to $16.8 billion, a deceleration from the second quarter when revenue surged more than 140%.
  • The company reported an 11% adjusted operating margin year-to-date and positive third-quarter cash flow, generating $1.70 in revenue per dollar spent on computing power.
  • Anthropic materially curbed model distillation by rival labs, which accounted for 5% to 7% of revenue and will drag on fourth-quarter figures.
  • Marketing for the planned share sale could begin the week of November 9, 2026, targeting a listing before Thanksgiving.

Current and prospective investors in Anthropic are reconsidering the company's prospective public valuation, discussing a target of around $1.5 trillion ahead of its planned November stock market debut. According to a Reuters report, that number marks a notable step down from earlier expectations of roughly $2 trillion, whereas Bloomberg reported some prospective investors had floated a valuation between $1.8 trillion and $2 trillion. The shift follows a closed-door investor session held on October 14, 2026, at Anthropic's San Francisco headquarters.

Details from attendees reveal that the reception inside the room was mixed. Although the top-line figures confirmed surging demand and positive cash generation, some investors voiced disappointment that quarter-over-quarter expansion slowed substantially compared to earlier in the year.

Decelerating Growth Trims Valuation Expectations

The revision in valuation discussions stems directly from Anthropic's growth trajectory between quarters. Anthropic disclosed that third-quarter revenue increased 45% over the second quarter to reach $16.8 billion, according to The Next Web. In contrast, the company had more than doubled its business in the second quarter, when revenue leaped more than 140% from roughly $4.7 billion in the first quarter to $11.6 billion.

That rapid deceleration led several participants at the meeting to argue that underwriters must recalibrate listing expectations. As reported by Dealroom, some attendees suggested the valuation could even dip below $1.5 trillion. Anthropic was valued privately at $965 billion in May 2026, meaning that a $1.5 trillion initial public offering would still deliver substantial gains over its last private round while acknowledging public market discipline.

Margins, Compute Efficiency, and Distillation Cuts

Beyond raw revenue, Anthropic's leadership—including chief executive Dario Amodei and chief financial officer Krishna Rao—presented financial efficiency metrics to support their profitability case. Notes compiled by Simon Willison's Weblog show that Anthropic generated $1.70 in revenue for every dollar spent on computing capacity in the third quarter.

Financial disclosures shared with investors showed an adjusted operating margin of 11% year-to-date through the third quarter, excluding stock-based compensation costs. Anthropic also told prospective buyers that it operated cash-flow positive during the three months ending in September, as noted by CNBC. These metrics contrast with full-year 2025 performance, during which the business generated nearly $4.6 billion in revenue but racked up an operating loss exceeding $8 billion.

However, another revenue factor gave investors pause: model distillation. Anthropic informed attendees that rival artificial intelligence developers paying to train their systems on Claude outputs had accounted for 5% to 7% of Anthropic's revenue. The company disclosed that it clamped down on this activity, cutting it back materially during the third quarter. Anthropic indicated this clampdown would continue to weigh on fourth-quarter figures, while Simon Willison noted via napkin math that other labs spent over half a billion dollars distilling Anthropic's models in the second quarter.

Broader Market Headwinds and Debut Timeline

The pricing debate comes amid broader tremors in public markets. Global tech equities have faced selling pressure that hit semiconductor makers, neocloud hosting providers, and related infrastructure firms. Shares and debt issued by CoreWeave declined sharply on October 15, reflecting rising unease about capital expenditure programs funded by heavy borrowing, according to Investing.com.

Other public offerings in energy and infrastructure have also stumbled. Data center operator SB Energy delayed its share sale in September, while Holtec Nuclear withdrew its planned listing and Australian data firm Firmus Grid canceled its public offering. Anthropic has committed to spend at least $518 billion over a decade on cloud computing infrastructure, leaving its long-term financial stability closely tethered to capital market liquidity.

Despite these market headwinds, the listing's timing has shifted more than once, having previously been expected to take place in October before the midterms. Formal roadshow marketing could commence as soon as the week of November 9, 2026, with public trading targeted before Thanksgiving on November 26, though sources caution that schedule details remain fluid.

Questions people ask

Why are investors discussing a lower IPO valuation for Anthropic?

Investors lowered valuation targets from $2 trillion to around $1.5 trillion because sequential revenue growth slowed from over 140% in Q2 to 45% in Q3, alongside a broader market retreat in tech and AI infrastructure stocks.

What were Anthropic's third-quarter 2026 financial results?

Anthropic reported Q3 revenue of $16.8 billion, achieved adjusted operating profitability, generated positive cash flow, and earned $1.70 in revenue for every $1 spent on computing power.

What is model distillation and why is it impacting Anthropic's outlook?

Distillation involves other AI companies training their models on Anthropic's outputs. It previously made up 5% to 7% of Anthropic's revenue, but the company materially restricted the practice in Q3, which will reduce fourth-quarter revenue.

When is Anthropic scheduled to go public?

Formal marketing is expected to start as early as the week of November 9, 2026, with trading potentially beginning before Thanksgiving on November 26, though dates remain subject to change.
