Apple Partners With Alibaba to Power Apple Intelligence in China

Alibaba's Qwen large language model will handle text, document, and image workflows across Siri, Writing Tools, and Visual Intelligence in China starting in December.

In short

  • Apple and Alibaba confirmed a partnership on October 31, 2026, integrating Alibaba's Qwen model into Apple Intelligence features in China.
  • The integration supports Siri queries, system-wide Writing Tools generation, and camera-based Visual Intelligence.
  • Testing opens November 4, 2026, in the iOS developer beta, with public availability scheduled for December alongside iOS 27.3.
  • Alibaba stated that user queries and generated outputs will not be used to train its AI models, and user opt-in is required.
  • China's cyberspace authority completed generative AI regulatory filings for smartphone services, including Apple Intelligence, in July 2026.

Apple announced on October 31, 2026, that it is bringing its first set of Apple Intelligence features to mainland China in partnership with Alibaba Group. Under the agreement, Alibaba's Qwen large language model family will integrate directly into Apple's operating system to handle complex generative tasks and image comprehension, according to Apple Newsroom.

The integration marks the first time Apple Intelligence has officially landed in mainland China since Apple unveiled the system at its Worldwide Developers Conference in 2024. Developer beta testing for the features begins on November 4, 2026, followed by a general public rollout in December with the release of iOS 27.3, as reported by Xinhua.

How Qwen Integrates Into Siri, Writing Tools, and Visual Features

Under the partnership, Qwen acts as a third-party intelligence provider embedded directly into native iOS interfaces, eliminating the need for users to open a standalone application. When users ask Siri questions that require broad knowledge, Siri can route the request to Qwen. Users can query Qwen about photos as well as documents such as PDFs and presentations, according to IT Home.

Beyond Siri, Qwen provides text generation within the system-wide Writing Tools, allowing users to compose original content from scratch. Alibaba's models will also assist Visual Intelligence, which enables users to point their iPhone camera at real-world objects and places to retrieve contextual information, Türkiye Today reported.

Users can access Qwen by signing in with an existing Qwen account or by using Sign in with Apple. Alibaba confirmed that users retain full control over whether to enable the integration and what data to send, adding that user prompts and generated responses will not be used to train Alibaba's models.

Core System Capabilities in the Initial Release

Alongside Qwen integration, Apple's proprietary on-device and cloud models will handle several foundational tasks. As detailed by Apple Newsroom, the release introduces live translation embedded into Messages, FaceTime, and the Phone app, as well as face-to-face spoken translation through supported AirPods models.

System utilities also receive generative features, including Clean Up in the Photos app to remove background distractions, natural language photo search, and automatic memory movie creation. In Mail, a Priority Mail section surfaces time-sensitive messages such as same-day invitations and boarding passes, complemented by one-tap email summaries. Notifications gain summarized lock screen stacks and Reduce Interruptions focus modes, while voice recordings in Notes and the Phone app generate automatic transcript summaries.

According to IT Home, this initial rollout focuses on high-frequency utility features and does not include Siri AI or other next-generation Apple Intelligence capabilities unveiled at WWDC 2026.

Hardware Support, Platform Availability, and Regulatory Approvals

Access to the features requires an Apple Intelligence-compatible iPhone purchased in China, or a device with both location settings and Apple Account region set to mainland China, with system and Siri languages configured to English and Simplified Chinese. Compatible hardware includes iPhone 16 and subsequent models, iPhone Duo, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and iPhone Air. Live translation via audio requires AirPods 5, AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation, AirPods Pro 2 or later, or AirPods Max 2 paired with a supported device.

While the December release is limited to iOS 27.3, Alibaba previously confirmed that Qwen integrations are planned to expand across iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS once necessary approvals are granted, according to IT Home. Regulatory clearance represents a critical milestone: China's cyberspace administration completed generative AI filings for seven smartphone services, including Apple Intelligence, in July 2026, Xinhua reported.

Alibaba Group CEO Eddie Wu stated that Qwen will integrate into Apple's ecosystem to deliver AI-driven services to Chinese users. Wang Xiaojie, an AI specialist at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, told Xinhua that this marks the first instance of a leading global consumer hardware manufacturer adopting a domestic large model as its primary system-level AI backbone in China.

Questions people ask

When will Apple Intelligence with Alibaba Qwen launch in China?

Developer beta testing starts November 4, 2026, with public availability scheduled for December 2026 alongside the official release of iOS 27.3.

Which iPhone models support these features in China?

Supported models include iPhone 16 and subsequent models, iPhone Duo, iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, and iPhone Air. Devices must be set to English and Simplified Chinese.

Does Alibaba use Apple Intelligence data to train its AI?

No. Alibaba stated that user requests and generated responses handled through the Apple integration will not be used for model training.

Will Qwen support platforms beyond iOS?

Yes. Alibaba confirmed plans to extend Qwen integrations to iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS once regulatory approvals are secured.
