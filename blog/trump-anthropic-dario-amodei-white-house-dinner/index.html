Trump Hosts Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for White House Dinner

The private meeting marks the first one-on-one discussion between President Trump and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei amid ongoing debates over AI safety and defense access.

In short

  • President Donald Trump was scheduled to host Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for a private one-on-one dinner at the White House on September 27, 2026.
  • The meeting marks their first direct bilateral discussion following earlier friction over defense access, model safety, and a Pentagon supply-chain risk designation.
  • The dinner precedes a Tuesday meeting between Trump, House Speaker Mike Johnson, and tech CEOs regarding corporate responsibility in AI safety.

President Donald Trump was scheduled to host Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for a private one-on-one dinner at the White House on the evening of September 27, 2026. The meeting, first reported by Axios and noted by TNW, marks the first direct bilateral dialogue between the two figures following months of strained relations over artificial intelligence policy, safety guardrails, and national defense applications.

Anthropic’s ties with the executive branch have been subject to intense scrutiny across 2026. the dinner provided a direct channel between the White House and the leadership of one of the foremost frontier AI research labs.

Details of the Private White House Meeting

The Sunday dinner follows an earlier gathering at the White House. Amodei missed a state dinner held the previous week for Chinese President Xi Jinping, which other prominent tech leaders including OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Google’s Sundar Pichai attended. According to reporting from Axios cited by TNW, Amodei missed the state dinner due to a scheduling conflict, leading Trump to extend a personal invitation for Sunday's private meeting. However, a report from the Washington Examiner offered a conflicting account, stating that Amodei was originally excluded from the state dinner guest list before the private dinner was arranged.

The one-on-one dinner also comes just ahead of a broader policy gathering. On Tuesday, September 29, 2026, President Trump and House Speaker Mike Johnson are scheduled to meet with multiple tech CEOs at the White House, where talks are expected to center on industry responsibilities for AI safety.

Navigating Past Defense and Safety Clashes

Relations between Anthropic and the Trump administration have faced multiple hurdles over the past year. Earlier in 2026, the Department of Defense designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk after the AI lab placed restrictions around how its technology could be deployed, as reported by TechCrunch. Anthropic has since been contesting that supply-chain designation in court.

Friction also flared regarding model access and distribution. According to the Washington Examiner, tensions grew after Amodei refused to grant the Pentagon unfettered access to Anthropic's flagship models. Additionally, in April 2026, the White House actively opposed Anthropic's plan to expand access to its Mythos model, as documented by TNW.

Broader obstacles facing the company under the administration have included export restrictions, scrutiny over safety philosophies, and criticism directed at Anthropic's connections to the effective altruism movement, according to analysis by Seeking Alpha.

Diverging Approaches to AI Development and Policy

The White House dinner highlights stark differences in how Amodei and the administration view the timeline and trajectory of AI deployment. In September 2026, Amodei called on the entire AI sector to decelerate and proceed with heightened caution. Trump dismissed calls to slow the technology, characterizing AI backlash as a Democratic hoax and advocating for a focus on what he terms "super intelligence," according to TechCrunch.

Reiterating the administration's stance, a White House official stated to Axios that "President Trump has been clear: America will lead the world in Super Intelligence, while protecting American consumers," as reported by TNW.

Despite advice from some senior administration officials against holding the meeting, the dinner signals an ongoing effort to maintain communication. Prior signs of easing tensions occurred in June 2026 during the G7 summit, after which Trump stated he no longer regarded Anthropic as a national security threat. As the administration actively formulates AI regulations, government partnerships, and defense alignments, the Sunday dialogue marks a pivotal point for Anthropic's regulatory standing and broader U.S. AI strategy.
