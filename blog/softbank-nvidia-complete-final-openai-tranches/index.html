SoftBank and Nvidia Complete Final Tranches in OpenAI Funding Round

The final $10 billion payments fulfill respective $30 billion commitments to the ChatGPT maker, cementing major tech backing.

Key takeaways

  • SoftBank Group executed its third and final $10 billion investment tranche in OpenAI on October 1, 2026, via SoftBank Vision Fund 2.
  • Nvidia also closed its final $10 billion tranche as part of a combined $20 billion capital injection across both firms.
  • SoftBank's cumulative investment in OpenAI has reached $64.6 billion, granting it an ownership interest of approximately 13%.
  • SoftBank financed its final tranche using proceeds from an $11.1 billion foreign currency-denominated corporate bond issuance after retiring its earlier bridge loan facility.

SoftBank Group and Nvidia have completed their final $10 billion investment tranches into OpenAI, fulfilling their respective $30 billion commitments to the artificial intelligence company. According to a press release from SoftBank Group, the Japanese conglomerate executed its third $10 billion tranche on October 1, 2026, through SoftBank Vision Fund 2. Breaking The News reported that The Information cited a person familiar with the matter saying Nvidia similarly delivered its final $10 billion payment on the same day, resulting in a combined $20 billion capital injection into the ChatGPT developer.

The completion of these tranches concludes the multi-stage financing agreements originally struck earlier in the year.

Structure of the Funding and SoftBank's Stake

Under the terms finalized in February 2026, SoftBank agreed to deploy $30 billion into OpenAI across three separate $10 billion installments, according to Unite.AI. The initial tranche closed on April 1, 2026, followed by a second payment on July 1, 2026, and the final installment on October 1, 2026. The investment was priced at a pre-money valuation of $730 billion, acquiring preferred shares that convert automatically into common stock if OpenAI completes an initial public offering.

With the final payment completed, SoftBank's total cumulative investment in OpenAI stands at $64.6 billion, representing an ownership interest of approximately 13%, as reported by Dow Jones Newswires via Morningstar. Prior to this $30 billion follow-on program, SoftBank had previously directed $34.6 billion into OpenAI through Vision Fund 2 beginning in September 2024.

OpenAI's broader fundraising round secured $122 billion in aggregate commitments, valuing the Sam Altman-led firm at $852 billion, according to reporting by Reuters via The Star. The round was anchored by major industry partners including Amazon, Nvidia, and SoftBank.

Debt Restructuring and Bridge Facility Repayment

To manage the heavy capital requirements of the three tranches, SoftBank initially relied on a $40 billion uncollateralized bridge loan facility established in March 2026 with a syndicate of lenders including JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs, Mizuho Bank, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, and MUFG Bank, according to Unite.AI.

SoftBank subsequently moved to refinance the debt. On September 9, 2026, the firm announced plans to prepay its outstanding bridge balance of $25.9 billion on September 15. On September 24, 2026, SoftBank priced $11.1 billion in foreign currency-denominated senior notes across dollar and euro denominations with coupon rates ranging from 7.125% to 9.750%.

According to official disclosures, SoftBank used the proceeds from the bond offering to fund the final $10 billion payment to OpenAI. Effective September 30, 2026, SoftBank formally canceled the remaining $10 billion in undrawn bridge capacity, leaving all facility borrowings fully repaid and no outstanding undrawn commitments remaining under the agreement.

Strategic Importance for AI Development

SoftBank Chairman and CEO Masayoshi Son previously stated that the funding is intended to accelerate OpenAI's research while aligning with SoftBank's artificial superintelligence strategy, as reported by Unite.AI.
