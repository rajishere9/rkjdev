The short version

  • The website has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies of its own.
  • Browser games save your progress only in your own browser. It is never sent to us.
  • If you email us, we use your email only to reply to you.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.
  • Each app or game we publish has its own policy covering what that app collects.

The website

Information we collect. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details on the website, and we do not run analytics, ad networks or tracking pixels on it.

Hosting. rkjdev.com is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may log it for security and to keep the service running. We do not receive these logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Fonts. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address and browser details with Google. Google states that Google Fonts does not set cookies. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ and the Google Privacy Policy.

Links to other sites. Blog articles and other pages link to outside sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Browser games (Play)

Games at rkjdev.com/play, such as AI-dle and Night Shift, store your progress, scores and settings in your browser's local storage on your device. This data stays in your browser, is never sent to us and is not linked to you. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include. We use it only to respond and to keep a record of the conversation, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that. Email is handled by Google (Gmail) under the Google Privacy Policy.

Apps and games we publish

Our apps and games can use services such as advertising and in-app purchases, which collect information this website does not. Each one has its own privacy policy with the details, linked from its page and its store listing. Where an app's own policy is more specific, it applies to that app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with the service providers named in this policy as needed to run the website and our apps, or when the law requires us to.

Children

The website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, to withdraw consent and to raise a complaint. Email rajankrishnan909@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Questions or complaints about how we handle data go to the same address, handled by the proprietor, Rajan Krishnan.

Security

The website is served only over HTTPS. Because we keep almost no personal information, there is very little for us to lose; the email we do hold is protected by the security of our email provider's account.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website and our apps change. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
