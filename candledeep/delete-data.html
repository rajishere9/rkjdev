Delete your CandleDeep data

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This page is for players of CandleDeep, the Android game published on Google Play by rkj dev (RKJDEV Labs). It explains how to delete your data, what is deleted and what is kept.

The short version

CandleDeep has no accounts and no servers of its own, so we hold no data about you. Your progress is saved only on your phone, and you can delete it yourself in a few seconds. If you would like us to confirm this or help, email rajankrishnan909@gmail.com.

How to delete your data

  1. Reset your progress in the game

    Open CandleDeep and go to Settings > Reset progress. This deletes your save from the phone: gold, gems, heroes, settings and statistics. It cannot be undone.

  2. Or uninstall the game

    Uninstalling CandleDeep removes the game and its save from your phone. If Android backup is switched on, a copy of the save may be kept in your Google account backup. You can manage or delete device backups in your Google account.

  3. Reset your advertising ID

    The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. To reset or delete it, or to opt out of ads personalization, open your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).

  4. Or ask us by email

    Write to rajankrishnan909@gmail.com with the subject CandleDeep data deletion. You do not need to send any ID or account details. We reply within 3 business days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhere it isWhat happens
Game progressOnly on your phone: gold, gems, heroes, settings and statistics.Deleted when you reset your progress or uninstall the game.
Android backupIn your Google account, if Android backup is switched on.Kept by Google until you delete the backup in your Google account.
Advertising dataWith Google, collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.Handled and retained under Google's policies. Resetting your advertising ID separates future ads from it.
Purchase recordsWith Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.Kept by Google Play in your order history under Google's policies.
Emails you send usIn our mailbox.Deleted when no longer needed to answer you, or sooner if you ask.

How long we keep data

We hold no game data about you on our side, so there is nothing for us to retain. The only personal information we ever receive is an email you choose to send, and we delete it on request. Data held by Google is retained under the Google Privacy Policy.

More detail

The full picture of what the game collects is in the CandleDeep Privacy Policy. Questions about this page: rajankrishnan909@gmail.com.
