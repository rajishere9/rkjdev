About the game

Beneath every grave lies the Hollow. You wake at its gate with one candle of time. Survive the endless horde until the candle burns out, carry your gold back up, and descend again stronger. CandleDeep is played with one hand in portrait: move with the joystick while your weapons fire on their own, and choose an upgrade at every level-up.

  • Free on Google Play (Android 7.0 and newer). Plays fully offline.
  • 2 stages, 20 enemies, 8 weapons with evolutions, 7 heroes, 23 achievements.
  • Rated ESRB Everyone 10+ / PEGI 7.

Questions

What kind of game is CandleDeep?

CandleDeep is a pixel-art roguelike survivor game (also called a bullet heaven or survivors-like) for Android. You move with one thumb, your weapons attack automatically, and at every level-up you choose an upgrade. Each run lasts up to 10 minutes: survive until the candle burns out, bring your gold back and grow stronger for the next run.

Is CandleDeep free?

Yes. CandleDeep is free on Google Play. Ads are optional rewards (revive, double gold), an ad may appear between some runs but never during one, and a single purchase removes all ads. A free PC, Mac, Linux and browser version is on itch.io.

Does CandleDeep work offline?

Yes. CandleDeep plays fully offline with no Wi-Fi or mobile data, and it has no account or login. Your progress is saved on your phone.

Which phones can run CandleDeep?

Any Android phone or tablet on Android 7.0 or newer. The game is small, plays in portrait with one hand and is tuned to run on older and low-end phones.

Is CandleDeep like Vampire Survivors?

It is in the same genre: auto-attacking weapons, a growing horde, level-up choices and weapon evolutions. CandleDeep is its own game, built for phones and one-handed portrait play, with its own world (the Hollow beneath the graves), heroes, bosses such as the Slime King, the Scarab Queen and the Pharaoh, plus a Daily Candle challenge, altars and Ledger Pacts.

How much content is there?

2 stages (the Dungeon Depths and the Sunken Tomb) with 3 difficulty tiers each, 20 enemies including bosses and champion enemies, 8 weapons that each evolve, 8 passive items, 7 heroes, 13 permanent power-ups, 23 achievements and a daily challenge.

Who makes CandleDeep?

CandleDeep is made by RKJDEV Labs, an independent one-person studio run by Rajan Krishnan in India.

Purchases

  • Missing a purchase? Open the game, go to Settings > Restore purchases. Items you paid for (Remove Ads, Gold Doubler, Starter Pack) come back on any device signed in to the same Google account.
  • Payment still pending? Some payment methods take a while. The item is granted automatically once Google Play confirms the payment, the next time you open the game.
  • Refunds and cancellations: see the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Ads

Rewarded ads are optional. An ad may appear between runs, never during one. Buying Remove Ads removes all ads and makes ad rewards free. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent at any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.

Your data and deleting it

CandleDeep has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved only on your phone. To delete it, use Settings > Reset progress or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google's ads SDK is handled under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Privacy > Ads. To ask us about any data, email the address above. Step-by-step instructions are on the data deletion page, and details are in the privacy policy.
