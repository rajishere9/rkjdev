Night Shift

It's 3am at a datacenter in the middle of nowhere, and something is using the cluster, 17 minutes at a time. A short mystery with seven puzzles and three endings. It takes about half an hour and saves as you go.

Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, and hold Shift to hurry. Space talks and uses things, Esc goes back, Tab opens your pager. You can also click where you want to go.Walk with the pad, or tap where you want to go. Tap people and things to use them. A uses, B goes back, and holding B while you walk hurries you along.

Every pixel and every sound is made in code as you play: no image or audio files. Sound is best with headphones.
