        How to play

        Every day there's one subject from the week's AI news: a company or lab, or an AI model or product.

        1. You start with one clue. The other five are redacted.
        2. Type a name and pick it from the list. Each wrong guess, or a skip, declassifies the next clue.
        3. Wrong guesses get a note in the margin: same country as the subject or same maker means you're close.

        Six clues, six guesses. A new case opens at midnight, your time.
